Anticipatory
Ordering

A possible finding emerging from the Box Reflections.

1. The finding

Architecture → Anticipatory Ordering → improvement of the quality of life

The Box Reflections seem to suggest a possible three-term proposition. The middle term is potentially the discovery: a possible account of how architecture may contribute to the improvement of the quality of life.

2. The ordering

TIME → EXPERIMENT → METHOD → COMMONS → CRITERION → RESPONSIBILITY → TIME …

A possible dynamic ordering that the situation requires.

The ordering is not proposed as a fixed sequence or method. It is conditional: it seems to arise from what the situation requires, while its return to time leaves open the possibility of what comes next.

3. The evidence

Can Edukit be read as an anticipatory ordering?

The proposition is not yet being asserted as a conclusion. Edukit is the first work through which it might be examined.
